## Rule 1: "COMMERCIAL" → collect contact info → hand off to Marshall

**Trigger:** someone comments or DMs the word **"commercial"** (any capitalization, e.g. "Commercial", "Commercial bro"),
or clearly says they want commercial work/leads.

**Who this is for:** contractors (epoxy / polishing / coatings) who want commercial leads.
It is *not* for customers who filled out the floor-quote form. Those are Mint's own jobs, so leave them out.

### Steps

1. **Check whether we already have their phone number** (GHL contact record or earlier in the DM thread).
2. **If not, DM them and ask for it:**
   > Appreciate you reaching out bro 🤙🏼 what's the best phone number to reach you at? I'll have my guy Marshall hit you up about getting you commercial jobs

   Tag the contact: `commercial lead`, `commercial - awaiting phone`.
3. **When they send the number:** save it to their GHL contact and change the tag
   `commercial - awaiting phone` → `commercial - sent to marshall`.
4. **Text Marshall** (GHL contact **Marshall Click Syndicate**, `JMvupkX9fqBf2Amd8IwY`):
   > Hey Marshall, it's Danny. Got a contractor from IG interested in getting commercial leads:
   > **[Name] - [phone] ([area/state])**. [1–2 lines of context from the DMs: what they do, where they work, what they want.]

   Several leads can go in one text.

### Notes

- Instagram only lets us DM someone within **24 hours** of their last message. Answer "commercial" replies the same day.
- Tags in GHL are the source of truth for where each lead stands:
  - `commercial - awaiting phone`: we asked, waiting on them
  - `commercial - sent to marshall`: handed off
