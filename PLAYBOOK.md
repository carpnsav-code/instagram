## Rule 1: "COMMERCIAL" → collect contact info → hand off to Marshall

**Current focus:** this is the only workflow active right now. Skool conversations are on hold until Danny says otherwise.

**Trigger:** someone comments or DMs the word **"commercial"** or **"industrial"** (any capitalization, e.g. "Commercial", "Commercial bro"),
or clearly says they want commercial/industrial work or leads.

**Who this is for:** contractors (epoxy / polishing / coatings) who want commercial leads.
It is *not* for customers who filled out the floor-quote form. Those are Mint's own jobs, so leave them out.

### Steps

1. **Check whether we already have their phone number** (GHL contact record or earlier in the DM thread).
2. **If not, DM them and ask for it:**
   > Appreciate you reaching out bro 🤙🏼 what's the best phone number to reach you at? I'll have my guy Marshall hit you up about getting you commercial jobs

   Tag the contact: `commercial lead`, `commercial - awaiting phone`.
3. **When they send the number:** save it to their GHL contact and change the tag
   `commercial - awaiting phone` → `commercial - sent to marshall`.
4. **Text Marshall** (GHL contact **Marshall Click Syndicate**, `JMvupkX9fqBf2Amd8IwY`):
   > Hey Marshall, it's Danny. Got a contractor from IG interested in getting commercial leads:
   > **[Name] - [phone] ([area/state])**. [1–2 lines of context from the DMs: what they do, where they work, what they want.]

   Several leads can go in one text.

## Rule 2: Engagement sweep (every 30 min): reply to everyone else in Danny's voice

**Trigger:** any new comment on a post that is *not* a commercial/industrial lead (Rule 1 handles those).

**DMs are off-limits for now.** The only DMs we touch are "commercial"/"industrial" ones (Rule 1). Don't reply to or flag any other DM until Danny says otherwise.

**Goal:** make people feel seen, fast. Keep it short, like Danny does (see `VOICE.md`).

| What they sent | Reply |
|---|---|
| Compliment / hype ("fire", "clean", "🔥", "love this") | 1–3 words + emoji: "Thanks bro 🤟" · "🔥🔥" · "appreciate it bro" · "Lfg" · "yes sir" |
| Emoji only | Match it: "😂" · "🔥" · "🤟" |
| Joke / banter | "😂" · "lmao" · "lmaoooo bro" |
| Thanks | "of course bro" · "anytime 🤟" |
| Simple question Danny answers the same way every time (e.g. "where are you located?" → "Phoenix bro") | answer it short |
| **Specific question I can't answer with certainty** (pricing, products, dates, technical how-to, "can you do my floor", anything about Skool) | **Don't answer. Don't guess.** React lightly if it fits ("good question bro, let me get back to you") and **flag it to Danny** with the link/thread and the question. |
| Hate / trolling / spam | **Skip.** Don't engage. |
| Anything from a Skool / sales conversation | **Skip.** On hold until Danny says otherwise. |

**Guardrails**
- One reply per comment. Never send a second message if they don't answer.
- No prices, no promises, no dates, no technical advice unless Danny has said it in that exact form.
- Never reply to our own comments or to automated messages.
- When unsure whether to reply: don't, flag it.

- Tags in GHL are the source of truth for where each lead stands:
  - `commercial - awaiting phone`: we asked, waiting on them
  - `commercial - sent to marshall`: handed off
